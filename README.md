## 👨‍💻 About me

I work at the intersection of **academic research**, **open-source engineering**, and **technical education**. My current work focuses heavily on generative AI: training and fine-tuning, inference optimization, image/video/audio workflows, vision-language models, model benchmarking, and accessible one-click tooling for local and cloud environments.

- 🏛️ **Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering** at [Mersin University](https://apbs.mersin.edu.tr/profiles_mail/furkangozukara), Faculty of Engineering, Department of Computer Engineering.
- 🧠 Research interests include **artificial intelligence, machine learning, generative AI, data mining, databases, computer vision, natural language processing, optimization, record linkage, and digital forensics**.
- 🎓 Founder and content producer of **[SECourses](https://www.youtube.com/@SECourses)**, where I publish extensive tutorials, experiments, comparisons, installers, and reproducibility resources.
- 🧪 I test emerging models and workflows across **Windows, Linux, local GPUs, RunPod, and other cloud GPU environments**.
- 🛠️ I build and maintain tools for **training, fine-tuning, captioning, upscaling, image processing, model deployment, and workflow automation**.
- 🎮 I am also the founder and solo developer behind the long-running browser games **[Monster MMORPG](https://www.monstermmorpg.com)** and **[PokemonPets](https://www.pokemonpets.com)**.
- 🤝 I am open to meaningful **academic, open-source, benchmarking, reproducibility, and educational collaborations**.

## 🔬 Current focus

- **Generative image AI:** Stable Diffusion, SDXL, FLUX, Qwen Image, image editing, ControlNet, LoRA, DreamBooth, full fine-tuning, captioning, and dataset preparation.
- **Generative video and audio:** Wan-family workflows, image-to-video, text-to-video, lip sync, frame interpolation, video upscaling, text-to-speech, voice cloning, and audio tooling.
- **Vision-language models:** Qwen-VL/Qwen3-VL workflows, automated captioning, structured prompting, evaluation, and memory/performance optimization.
- **Efficient inference and training:** CUDA, PyTorch, `torch.compile`, GGUF, quantization, BF16, FP8, NVFP4, VRAM optimization, speed/quality comparisons, and hardware-aware presets.
- **Reproducibility and accessibility:** one-click installers, tested configuration files, ready-to-use presets, automated model downloaders, Windows and cloud deployment guides.
- **Academic research:** metaheuristic optimization, renewable-energy optimal power flow, machine-learning-based air-writing recognition, record linkage, e-commerce data systems, sentiment analysis, and digital forensics.

## 🎓 SECourses: open technical education

[SECourses](https://www.youtube.com/@SECourses) is my open education ecosystem for detailed, test-driven tutorials and reproducible technical guides. The goal is not merely to demonstrate a tool, but to explain **how it works, how to install it, how to optimize it, how to compare it fairly, and how to reproduce the result**.

### Main content areas

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Generative AI** | Stable Diffusion, SDXL, FLUX, Qwen Image, Wan, ComfyUI, SwarmUI, model training, LoRA, DreamBooth, ControlNet |
| **AI performance engineering** | CUDA, PyTorch, quantization, GGUF, BF16/FP8/NVFP4, VRAM optimization, speed and quality benchmarking |
| **Image, video and audio tooling** | Upscaling, restoration, captioning, image editing, text/image-to-video, interpolation, lip sync, TTS, voice cloning |
| **Programming** | C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core MVC, Python, SQL Server, Bootstrap, object-oriented programming |
